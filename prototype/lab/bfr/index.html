    What to take away

    Detection is not “N bad samples in a row”. Each BFI restarts the timer and adds one to the counter. A quiet period does not clear the counter unless it lasts long enough for the timer to expire. BFIs at 20, 40, 80 and 100 ms reach a count of 4 even though 60 ms was quiet.

    Counterexample. BFIs at 20 and 40 ms, then silence. The timer, restarted at 40 ms with 80 ms to run, expires at 120 ms and resets the counter. A BFI at 160 ms starts again from 1.

    Detection ≠ recovery. Reaching the max count only starts random access on the SpCell. Finding a candidate beam, or sending the preamble, is not recovery. Recovery is complete when a PDCCH addressed to the UE's C-RNTI arrives on recoverySearchSpace within ra-ResponseWindow. That success sets BFI_COUNTER to 0 and stops beamFailureRecoveryTimer, the recovery timer, not the detection timer. Detection-timer expiry is a separate way the counter resets.

    Failure at a gate is not RLF. If the dedicated resources are not eligible, MAC carries on with other random-access resource selection, such as contention-based RA. A missed response leads to the normal retry handling. Neither one is, by itself, radio link failure, and BFR is not handover: the UE stays on the same SpCell.

    Model scope and limits

    Baseline: one active SpCell, non-DRX, one BFD-RS set and a 4-step RA. Only the dedicated contention-free BFR branch is simulated. Contention-based fallback, preamble retransmission, power ramping, back-off, SCell BFR (which uses a BFR MAC CE and SR, not this PRACH path), PSCell with a deactivated SCG, two-TRP BFD-RS sets and NTN are named but not modelled. The 20 ms period, the −100 dBm threshold and the recovery timing (preamble +6 ms, window +10 to +30 ms, response +18 ms after detection) are illustrative inputs, not normative latencies.

    After detection the BFI row is frozen. Later BFIs, the later run of beamFailureDetectionTimer and any counter reset by its expiry are not simulated. The only post-detection counter change drawn is the reset on successful recovery, and the timer is drawn faded. Tie convention: if a BFI lands exactly on the timer deadline, this model counts the BFI first, which restarts the timer. So pbfdN tolerates up to N−1 quiet periods. The specification text does not order these two events. Ties are marked on the timeline.

    Candidate selection: the MAC selects an eligible candidate above the threshold, not necessarily the strongest. The page shows one candidate value for clarity. The BFI criterion (Qout,LR, a hypothetical PDCCH BLER) differs from the candidate criterion (L1-RSRP against a threshold).
